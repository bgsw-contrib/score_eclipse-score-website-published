    Backed by an industry-wide Memorandum of Understanding

    In June 2025, automotive companies signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), supported by the German Association of the Automotive Industry (VDA), for cooperation in open-source software development. This development takes place in the vendor-independent environment of the Eclipse Foundation as and target e.g. the S-CORE project. By January 2026, the MoU had grown to 32 companies across the global automotive value chain, with Eclipse S-CORE at the centre of the collaboration.

    HOW WE WORK

    Clear ownership, open collaboration

    S-CORE works in two parallel tracks. On the non-technical side, the X-CORE Platform Council provides strategy and guidance, steering the project without managing it. On the technical side, project leads, technical leads, and contributors define modules, propose requirements, and evolve the platform in open working groups.

    01

    Transparent decisions

    Direction is shaped in public, with proposals and outcomes visible to contributors.

    02

    Shared artefacts

    Specifications, code, and development knowledge build on one another across the ecosystem.

    03

    Defined responsibilities

    Council, leads, contributors, and working groups each have clear roles in their track.

    PROJECT GOVERNANCE

    Two lead circles. One shared direction.

    Within the technical track, two circles lead the project. Project leads represent their organizations and coordinate direction, planning, and resources. Technical leads guide architecture and technical alignment. Strategic steering comes from the X-CORE Platform Council.
