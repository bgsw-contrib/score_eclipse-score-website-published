Why Eclipse S-CORE

A shared foundation for vehicle software

Collaboration for a safer, faster automotive future.

Eclipse S-CORE unites OEMs, suppliers, and innovators around a common software runtime, reducing duplication and improving reuse across the software-defined vehicle stack.

Learn more →
Collaboration One common stack for shared industry problems.
Speed Code-centric, iterative development in the open shortens feedback loops and time to market.
Quality Many contributors from different disciplines review every change, and reuse across vehicle generations removes sporadic errors that only show up over time.
The platform

The shared core of the vehicle software stack

Eclipse S-CORE fully covers the needs of safety-critical domains such as ADAS, providing a reusable, qualifiable base for automotive functionality on high-performance ECUs.

Project specifics stay adoptable: series projects tailor and extend the distribution to their needs without forking the shared core.

S-CORE Values

Ten proof points define the S-CORE platform

  1. 01 Transparent

    First free and open-source software platform for safety-critical automotive HPCs.

  2. 02 Fast & reliable

    OSS works for automotive: from project definition to ready for adoption S-CORE in two years.

  3. 03 International

    A global community supports entry into the major automotive markets.

  4. 04 Free & OSS

    Developed openly by a community whose members contribute on equal footing.

  5. 05 Adoptable

    The distribution can be tailored to the needs of a specific series project.

  6. 06 Full stack

    A code-first approach covers the complete software platform, including operating system and middleware layers.

  7. 07 Complete

    Product artefacts and the full software-development process are open source.

  8. 08 Qualifiable

    The stack is designed to support safety qualification.

  9. 09 Low threshold

    Documented standards and an open toolchain lower the entry barrier.

  10. 10 AI enabled

    X-as-code creates the basis for automation and AI-assisted delivery.

S-CORE Values | Open, fast, global & ready for series adoption

IMPORTANT FOR SERIES PROGRAMS

A joint safety-ready core to start a series project

Eclipse S-CORE provides an open and joint software foundation. Each series project remains responsible for the compliance of its final system, including applicable functional-safety, cybersecurity, and process requirements.
